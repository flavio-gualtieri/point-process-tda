**Status key.** **EXISTS** means the numbers are already in a repo artifact and only need to be typeset.
**PARTIAL** means the inputs exist but a small aggregation or re-run over stored outputs is needed (no retraining).
**NOT FOUND** means it needs new analysis, a new plot, or a decision.
