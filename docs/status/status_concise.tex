\section{Summary}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Classical summary functions beat persistence images on every parameter-estimation family and on classification (accuracy 0.712 vs.\ 0.648).
  \item Adding $F$, $G$, $J$ to $L$ helps little: the best subset is within about one seed SD of $L$ alone for parameter estimation (except nested Thomas), and +2.6 points for classification.
  \item $H_0$ carries most of the persistence information; $H_1$ adds little on top.
  \item The PH gap depends on the regime. Parameter estimation: it is largest at intermediate distance from Poisson. Classification: it is largest near Poisson.
\end{itemize}

\section{Data and methods}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Models:} Poisson, Thomas, nested Thomas, Mat\'ern~II (hard-core), LGCP on the unit square, with expected count $\nbar \in [100, 800]$.
  \item \textbf{Distance from Poisson, $\dtil$:} the largest standardised deviation of the model's theoretical $L$-function from Poisson, divided by the 5\% critical value of the classical global envelope test. $\dtil = 0$ is Poisson; $\dtil \approx 1$ is the edge of classical detectability.
  \item \textbf{Sets:} 10{,}000 training patterns per model (fixed 85/15 train/validation split); 5{,}000 test patterns per model from the same prior. All results are on the test set, overall and by $\dtil$ band.
  \item \textbf{Classical:} 1-D CNN of Vihrs (2022) on summary functions plus the point count. All 15 subsets of $\{L,F,G,J\}$ were run; shown are $L$ (the published baseline) and the subsets that are ever best ($L{+}F$, $L{+}J$, $L{+}F{+}G$).
  \item \textbf{PH:} CNN on persistence images of the DTM filtration ($k=5$): $H_0$, $H_1$, or both as two channels.
  \item \textbf{Training:} early stopping on validation loss; mean $\pm$ SD over 10 seeds.
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}

  \item $H_1$ alone is weak. Adding it to $H_0$ helps a little on Thomas and LGCP and nothing on nested and Mat\'ern~II.
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}
  \item $L{+}F{+}G$ is the best classifier (0.712), tied with $L{+}J$ (0.711). $L$ alone: 0.686.
  \item PH $H_0{+}H_1$ is 6.4 points behind (0.648). The deficit is on Thomas ($-$7 points recall) and Mat\'ern~II ($-$22); PH is level on Poisson and LGCP.
  \item The accuracy gap is largest near Poisson ($-$20 points at $\dtil < 0.5$) and shrinks to $-$1 point at $\dtil \ge 8$. Near Poisson, PH labels 69\% of structured patterns as Poisson, vs.\ 48\% for $L{+}F{+}G$.

  \item $H_1$ alone is above chance (0.434) but the weakest classifier and detector.
\end{itemize}
