\begin{abstract}
\noindent Seven vihrs 1-D CNN arms (Vihrs 2022 architecture, best-validation checkpointing), differing only in which summary functions feed the network, were trained on the DV3 dataset (\code{docs/generation_procedure.tex}) for parameter estimation (per family) and 5-way classification, and evaluated for CSR detection at a calibrated 5\% size. All runs are complete over the target 10 seeds (9371--9380). This note summarises the three tasks, breaks results down by $\dtil$ (distance from CSR, in units of the 5\% critical value of a studentised $L$-envelope test) and $\nbar$ (expected point count), and gives two figures. Every number here is reproduced by \code{scripts/dv3\_matrix.py} from \code{results/} on disk and matches the ``DV3 classical'' sheet of \code{docs/status/experiment\_matrix.xlsx}; figures are written by \code{scripts/dv3\_report\_figures.py}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Arms}\label{sec:arms}

Every arm is the same 1-D CNN reading $n(x)$ (point count) plus a subset of the classical summary functions $L(r)-r$, $F(r)$, $G(r)$, $J(r)=\frac{1-G(r)}{1-F(r)}$ evaluated on a radius grid $r\in[0,r_{\max}]$. Two grid resolutions were tried for the multi-function arms ($r_{\max}\in\{0.25,0.08\}$); Table~\ref{tab:arms} lists all seven.

Evaluation uses three held-out sets: \textbf{A}, 5{,}000 patterns per family drawn from the prior (risk over the prior; used for the headline numbers and all $\dtil$/$\nbar$ breakdowns); \textbf{B}, a grid of fixed parameter cells $\times$ 400 replicates; \textbf{C}, sixteen-level ladders from each structured family down to exact CSR, plus CSR anchors (used for the detection task and, pooled, for the classification $\dtil$ breakdown at the low end). All values below are mean $\pm$ sample SD over the 10 seeds.

On thomas and nested, where training is stable (SD $\le0.011$), the gap is 0.04 (thomas, 0.177 vs.\ 0.137) and 0.09 (nested, 0.454 vs.\ 0.365) on $A$. PI~H0 shares L's regime profile: loss falls with $\dtil$, and at $\dtil\ge8$ on thomas it matches L (0.022 vs.\ 0.021). The gap is therefore concentrated near CSR. Matern2 and lgcp are bimodal across seeds. On matern2, 6 seeds reach $A$ = 0.046--0.054, but 4 stopped early (epochs 55--66) at 0.14--0.19. On lgcp, 7 seeds reach 0.244--0.251, but 3 are stuck at 0.578, the $n(x)$-only value. Even the good seeds trail L (matern2 0.018, lgcp 0.220), so the ordering does not depend on the failures, but the means above overstate the typical gap on these two families.

\section{Summary}\label{sec:summary}

Across all three tasks the same pattern holds: $L(r)-r$ alone is a strong baseline, adding $F$, $G$, $J$ (LFGJ) helps classification meaningfully (+2.5 pts balanced accuracy on $A$, widening with $\dtil$) but not detection or, mostly, parameter estimation; and the single-function G-only/F-only arms are consistently the weakest, with $F$-only specifically blind to matern2's hard-core scale in both parameter estimation and classification. The 0.25 vs.\ 0.08 radius grid choice is a non-factor everywhere it was tried. These are the classical-baseline numbers against which the persistence-based (PH) estimators/classifiers on the same DV3 splits should be compared.
