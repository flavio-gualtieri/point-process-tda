\section{Experiments}

\subsection*{Run matrix}
Six tasks: 5-way classification, and parameter estimation for each of the five families. Each run through both arms at every feature setting.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{PH arm}, 18 settings: 6 filtrations (Rips, alpha, DTM$_{5,10,15,20}$) $\times$ $\{H_0,\ H_1,\ H_0{+}H_1\}$
  \item \textbf{Classical arm}, 4 settings:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item $L$ on \texttt{fixed} alone (VIHRS feature set)
    \item $L,F,G,J$ all on \texttt{fixed}, stacked
    \item $L,F,G,J$ all on \texttt{sqrtn}, stacked
    \item $L$ on \texttt{fixed}, $F,G,J$ on \texttt{sqrtn} (per-function grids, one encoder each)
  \end{itemize}
  \item $6\times(18+4)=132$ configurations $\times$ 10 seeds $=1320$ runs, all complete
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Conduct}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Everything is evaluated on the same 2000 test $\theta$ per family, fixed before any run and untouched until evaluation
  \item Best epoch ranges 7--196 with median 34. Six runs of 1320 reached the 200-epoch cap, all of them Mat\'ern parameters and five of them on a 1-D $H_0$ input
  \item Seed sd of a marginal cell estimate is $0.001$--$0.004$ for accuracy, an order of magnitude below the $\theta$ bootstrap width
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Thomas, nested and LGCP improve with $\dt$ in both arms. LGCP improves strictly, while the two clustering families plateau at $\dt\approx0.4$--$0.6$
  \item On Mat\'ern the classical arm is flat over the whole range ($0.105$--$0.170$, no trend above $\dt\approx0.25$) while Rips $H_0$ falls $0.181\to0.054$

  \item Only Mat\'ern's gap widens with $\dt$. LGCP's and Thomas's are widest in the middle and close again at the extremes, where both arms are either uninformative or saturated.
\end{itemize}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Shape parameters get harder with $\nb$}, in both arms: $\dt$ is defined at $\nb$ and the draw is independent of it, so at fixed $\dt$ a larger $\nb$ means a weaker per-point departure
  \item \textbf{Poisson is the exception.} Its target is $\nb$ itself, and $0.079/0.061/0.043$ tracks $1/\sqrt{\nb}$ ($0.084/0.059/0.042$ at the bins' geometric centres) --- the MLE floor for $n\mid\nb$, reached by all 22 inputs
\end{itemize}

\section{Observations}

\begin{itemize}
  \item PH's wins on Mat\'ern above $\dt\approx0.4$
  \item Classical arm wins classification overall: $-0.046$ paired at best, ahead in every $\dt$ bin from $0.16$. With $L$ alone (VIHRS) the gap is $-0.023$, mostly LGCP recall ($0.288\to0.386$); the rest comes from $F,G,J$
  \item PH matches or beats classical on Thomas up to $\dt\approx1$ and ties on Poisson. Classical wins nested against Rips $H_0$ at mid $\dt$ ($0.403$ vs $0.279$ at $0.40$), Mat\'ern below saturation ($0.872$ vs $0.619$ at $0.25$) and LGCP at high $\dt$ ($0.773$ vs $0.474$). Below $\dt\approx0.6$ every method fails on Thomas (recall $0.03$--$0.19$ vs $0.25$ chance), calling $56$--$71\%$ of those patterns Poisson
  \item $H_1$ adds little: at most $+0.002$ over $H_0$, weakest input alone for every family and task
  \item Rips/alpha beat DTM on classification ($0.495$ vs $0.477$) and Mat\'ern; DTM wins only LGCP recall ($0.386$ vs $0.321$), the one density-field departure.
  By regime the split is sharper: DTM$_{10}$ leads Rips on nested in every $\dt$ bin and on LGCP from $\dt\approx0.25$ up, and trails it on Mat\'ern in every bin below saturation
  \item Low-$\dt$ bins are below chance ($0.25$): the modal prediction is Poisson ($68\%$ of the bottom bin, $59\%$ of the next) (excluded there by construction, it's a property of the prior, not the methods)
  \item Some targets seemingly carry no feature information: Poisson $\nb$ is $0.063$ for all 22 inputs ($\log n$ solves it); nested $\mu_1$ stays at its prior sd ($\approx0.397$); Thomas $\kappa$ and $\mu$ agree within $0.003$, being one target given $\kappa=\nb/\mu$
\end{itemize}
