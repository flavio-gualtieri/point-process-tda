\section{Experiments}

\subsection*{Run matrix}
Six tasks: 5-way classification, and parameter estimation for each of the five families. Each run through both arms at every feature setting.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{PH arm}, 18 settings: 6 filtrations (Rips, alpha, DTM$_{5,10,15,20}$) $\times$ $\{H_0,\ H_1,\ H_0{+}H_1\}$
  \item \textbf{Classical arm}, 4 settings:
  \begin{itemize}
    \item $L$ on \texttt{fixed} alone (VIHRS feature set)
    \item $L,F,G,J$ all on \texttt{fixed}, stacked
    \item $L,F,G,J$ all on \texttt{sqrtn}, stacked
    \item $L$ on \texttt{fixed}, $F,G,J$ on \texttt{sqrtn} (per-function grids, one encoder each)
  \end{itemize}
  \item $6\times(18+4)=132$ configurations $\times$ 10 seeds $=1320$ runs, all complete
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Conduct}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Everything is evaluated on the same 2000 test $\theta$ per family, fixed before any run and untouched until evaluation
  \item Best epoch ranges 7--196 with median 34. Six runs of 1320 reached the 200-epoch cap, all of them Mat\'ern parameters and five of them on a 1-D $H_0$ input
  \item Seed sd of a marginal cell estimate is $0.001$--$0.004$ for accuracy, an order of magnitude below the $\theta$ bootstrap width
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Regime coordinate}
Cells are 8 log-$\dt$ bins on $[0.1,4]$ and 3 log-$\nb$ bins on $[100,800]$, edges from the prior and shared by every family and method. Binning is on $\dt$ \emph{recomputed under the current null tables}, whose radius set keeps radii with at least $p=5$ expected CSR pairs. The prior was swept against the earlier $p=10$ tables, so the label of a fixed $\theta$ has moved, and by different amounts per family:

\smallskip
\begin{itemize}
  \item For the three clustering families the relabelling is a uniform $-1$--$2\%$ and the bins stay flat at $\approx250$ $\theta$
  \item Mat\'ern moves: median ratio $1.40$, peaking at $1.86$ near $\dt_{10}\approx0.8$ and back to $0.99$ in the top two bins. A hard core of radius $R<r_{\min}$ enters $\dt$ only through the pair deficit it leaves \emph{above} $r_{\min}$, diluted by the CSR count there (01, 02); dropping $p$ to 5 extends $R(n)$ below $R$ and the deficit is seen directly. At large $R$ the core is already inside $R(n)$ at either cutoff, so the label does not move
  \item Consequence: Mat\'ern's prior is no longer flat across $\dt$ bins. Cell estimates are unaffected --- each is read within its own bin --- but the Mat\'ern intervals are wide at $\dt=0.10$ (73 $\theta$) and tight at $1.59$ (588 $\theta$)
  \item The sweep itself is unchanged. The shape-rule floor in \texttt{configs/simulation} stays at 10 expected pairs: that is a choice about which scales are worth sweeping, deliberately not the test's cutoff
  \item Poisson has $\dt=0$ and falls in no $\dt$ bin. It appears in the marginal and $\nb$ rows only, so a $\dt$ cell of the classification task holds four families and chance in it is $0.25$
\end{itemize}

\section{Observations}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{PH's edge is Mat\'ern's hard core}, above $\dt\approx0.4$ only. Paired difference on $R$ changes sign there, interval excludes $0$; top bin $0.040$ vs $0.147$ ($3.7\times$). A forbidden spacing is a length $H_0$ records and $\hat K$ averages over
  \item \textbf{Estimation, not identification.} The classical arm recognises Mat\'ern earlier (recall $1.0$ by $\dt\approx0.6$, PH by $\approx1.0$) and stays ahead on $R$ until $\dt\approx0.4$; PH only overtakes where the family is already certain
  \item \textbf{Classification goes to the classical arm}: $-0.046$ paired at best, ahead in every $\dt$ bin from $0.16$. With $L$ alone (VIHRS) the gap is $-0.023$, mostly LGCP recall ($0.288\to0.386$); the rest comes from $F,G,J$
  \item \textbf{Where the classification gap lives.} Against Rips $H_0$ per family: nested in the middle ($0.403$ vs $0.279$ at $\dt=0.40$), Mat\'ern below saturation ($0.872$ vs $0.619$ at $0.25$), LGCP at the top ($0.773$ vs $0.474$). Thomas is level or behind until $\dt\approx1$, Poisson level throughout. Thomas below $\dt\approx0.6$ is where every method fails --- recall $0.03$--$0.19$ against $0.25$ chance, and $56$--$71\%$ of those patterns are called Poisson
  \item \textbf{$H_1$ does not pay.} At most $+0.002$ over $H_0$, weakest input alone for every family and task; in 2-D the loops are the complement of clusters already in $H_0$
  \item \textbf{Geometry vs density.} Rips/alpha beat DTM on classification ($0.495$ vs $0.477$) and Mat\'ern; DTM wins only LGCP recall ($0.386$ vs $0.321$), the one density-field departure. By regime the split is sharper: DTM$_{10}$ leads Rips on nested in every $\dt$ bin and on LGCP from $\dt\approx0.25$ up, and trails it on Mat\'ern in every bin below saturation
  \item \textbf{PH degrades with $\nb$, classical does not} ($0.509\to0.472$ vs $0.544\to0.533$): $\sqrt n$ rescaling makes persistence images $\nb$-invariant in geometry, not information. The per-family split locates it --- Mat\'ern recall goes $0.897\to0.780$ under Rips $H_0$ while the classical arm goes $0.886\to0.918$. Thomas falls in both arms and nested rises in both, so neither contributes to the gap
  \item \textbf{$\nb$ hurts estimation in both arms.} Pooled log-RMSE rises with $\nb$ for every clustering family and both arms (Thomas $0.79\to0.97$ classical, $0.88\to1.16$ Rips $H_0$): at fixed $\dt$ more points means a weaker per-point departure. Poisson inverts it because its target is $\nb$
  \item \textbf{Low-$\dt$ bins are below chance} ($0.25$ for four families): the modal prediction is Poisson ($68\%$ of the bottom bin, $59\%$ of the next), excluded there by construction --- a property of the prior, not the methods
  \item \textbf{Some targets carry no feature information}: Poisson $\nb$ is $0.063$ for all 22 inputs ($\log n$ solves it); nested $\mu_1$ stays at its prior sd ($\approx0.397$); Thomas $\kappa$ and $\mu$ agree within $0.003$, being one target given $\kappa=\nb/\mu$
  \item \textbf{The $p=10\to5$ relabelling moves Mat\'ern only} ($\dt$ up $1.4\times$ at the median, $1.9\times$ near $\dt\approx0.8$; the other three families within $2\%$), because a sub-$r_{\min}$ hard core is the one departure the cutoff hides. The Mat\'ern $\dt$ curves above are therefore read on an unevenly populated prior, $73$ to $588$ test $\theta$ per bin
\end{itemize}
