## Section 5. Fidelity (480 headline clouds, 5 simulation seeds)

- Skill: 1 = as good as the true model, 0 = no better than a Poisson fit. Mean ± s.d. over 5 simulation seeds (same fits); interval: bootstrap over clouds, seeds pooled.
